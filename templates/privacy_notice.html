Responsible practice and privacy contact

This draft concerns the Ponky Ramosolo practice presented on this website as Ramosolo / Le Sonto Medical. The legal responsible-party name, its relationship to Le Sonto, and the person authorised to handle privacy requests have not been confirmed.

Approved privacy contact: not yet supplied. No Information Officer or Deputy Information Officer is named or appointed by this website. The business card’s general contact details are not an approved privacy-request channel. The Professional Information page identifies the supplied material and its confirmation limits.

Required and optional information

The current enquiry form requires your name, email, phone, a listed enquiry category, a future preferred time and agreement to contact about that enquiry. Without these, this form cannot submit a request. Do not supply an ID copy, symptoms, diagnosis or clinical history here. Any alternative contact route must be confirmed with the practice.

Optional check-ins and contact requests in an enabled patient plan are voluntary. Reminder, administrative-contact, referral-sharing and patient-facing-plan sharing decisions are recorded separately by purpose and channel. There is no marketing opt-in bundled into appointment contact. Choosing not to submit an optional check-in does not automatically cancel care or change treatment.

The practice must approve and document the applicable grounds for each activity, including health information. Optional communication consent is not presented as the basis for every healthcare-processing activity.

Who may receive information

The application limits operational access by institution and care assignment. Assigned doctors/psychologists have their authorised clinical workflows. Nurses require assignment and the relevant care permission. Assistants arrange bookings and administrative tasks without default diagnosis/note access. Administrators manage operations; clinical access requires a separate explicit permission. Patients see their own permitted appointments, preferences, agreed actions and explicitly shared plan.

Authorised exports are checked server-side and audited. The approved notice still needs to identify the hosting and support operators, billing recipients, and the grounds for disclosures to care providers, facilities, medical aids or regulators.

See the Data Management Declaration for confirmed and unconfirmed providers and integrations.

Communication choices and withdrawal

Account and security notices cover verification, sign-in and security changes. Appointment email is optional and requires a current purpose-specific decision and verified destination. Optional phone verification and phone-change alerts use a separate service when enabled. Marketing email and SMS are disabled. Account/security and appointment messages carry no promotional content.

Assigned patients can review the available portal preferences and purpose-specific consent records. Choices include channel, verified destination, contact window and timezone where recorded. You may request a change or withdrawal for the relevant purpose/channel. The application preserves the decision history, suppresses affected unsent optional notices and rechecks queued permissions; it does not retract a communication already delivered.

Withdrawal of an optional contact permission leaves necessary account and security messages available. Consent withdrawal does not automatically erase care records, cancel appointments or change treatment. Optional patient check-ins show the monitoring hours recorded in the agreed plan. Online requests are not an emergency service.

Access, correction, objection and deletion requests

Proposed process for owner approval; the approved request contact is pending:

  1. Ask the practice for its approved privacy contact before sending records or identity documents. State the type of request and a safe way to contact you; keep the initial message minimal.
  2. The authorised handler must verify identity and any authority to act for someone else using a proportionate, approved method and secure channel. A name, booking, relationship or shared phone number alone is not proof of authority. No identity-document upload or verification-by-email process is created here.
  3. The handler must check the relevant records, other people's information and any applicable access/retention restrictions, then arrange an authorised response or correction. A request is not automatic permission to export another patient's information.
  4. Deletion or restriction requests must be assessed separately from information that must be retained. The approved process must explain the decision and complaint route. No retention period, response deadline or automatic deletion promise is set by this draft.

The Information Regulator provides access-to-record guidance and forms and POPIA objection/correction/deletion forms. Those resources do not appoint the practice’s Information Officer.

Children, family members and caregivers

An approved child/guardian-consent and access policy has not been provided. The patient is the person receiving care. An enquirer or account holder may be a different person. Before a representative can act, the practice must verify their authority for the specific purpose and consider the child's capacity, wishes, safety and confidentiality under an approved process. A booking enquiry creates no authority or record access.

Reading a privacy notice explains how information is handled. Agreement to receive an enquiry response, treatment consent, marketing consent and evidence of a representative's authority are separate decisions. Guardian portal access is inactive.

See the Information Regulator's children’s information guidance, the Children’s Act and the National Health Act.

Questions and complaints

You can use the Information Regulator’s official complaints guidance and eServices portal for the current process. Follow the Regulator's instructions for the type of complaint; this website does not submit a complaint for you. Professional-conduct concerns have a separate HPCSA complaints resource.

The practice’s internal privacy contact, escalation handler and request procedures remain approval items. Nothing here restricts your ability to seek the applicable official complaint route.
