Roles and permissions

Every permission also requires an active medical-institution membership and patient scope. Account management never grants clinical access. Unknown permissions are denied.

Each clinical resource requires a separate patient-specific grant from that patient's assigned clinician. Read grants confer no clinical authorship. Export requires patient-specific export authorization plus permission to read each resource.

Clinical grants expire in effect when revoked, membership or authorizing clinician is disabled, the treating doctor changes, or a nurse's care assignment is removed. Account administrators cannot grant themselves clinical permissions. Referenced treating psychiatrists have no login or portal rights.

Sensitive views, drill-downs, exports and changes record actor, record ID, action and timestamp in the activity log. Clinical text stays in authorized records, outside activity logs.

Connected care coordination

Doctors sign off discharge planning, actual care-plan reviews and referral completion for assigned patients. Nurses record contacts and escalate only their assigned or covering tasks; this grants no diagnostic authority. Doctor assistants track administrative referral and scheduling states. Admins oversee operational status and assignment but cannot sign off clinical work. Private referral and review content requires clinical-note permission. Patients see only explicitly shared actions and a published plan with purpose-specific sharing consent. The primary clinician may issue time-limited, workflow-specific covering-clinician assignments. These confer no general diagnosis or psychotherapy access and admins cannot issue them. New modules offer no attachments or external transmissions.
