Consent and contact preferences

Each decision applies to one purpose, channel and scope. Leaving a field unanswered never grants consent. Withdrawing optional communication consent does not cancel care or decide the lawful basis for healthcare processing. No caregiver or family access is granted.

Phone, email and SMS destinations need a staff-recorded verification. Patient-entered destination changes remain unverified. Sharing permissions and clinical-record access are separate.
